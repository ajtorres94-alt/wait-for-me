Common questions

How do I find my waiter?

Open the line in My lines. You'll see your waiter's name, avatar, and what they're wearing.

Where's my swap code?

It's on your line in My lines. Read it to your waiter in person at the front of the line. Never share it before you're there.

How much will I pay?

You pay for the time your waiter spends in line, from check-in to swap, in 15-minute steps with a 1-hour minimum, plus a 15% service fee. It never goes above the time you approved.

Why is there a hold on my card?

When a waiter is booked and your line starts within 5 days, we place a temporary hold for the most the job could cost. When the job ends, we charge only for the time your waiter actually spent in line (1-hour minimum, plus the 15% service fee) and release the rest. Your bank may take a few days to remove the released amount from your statement. Charges show up as WAITFORME.

How do I get paid as a waiter?

Go to Account → Set up payouts. Stripe, our payment processor, asks for your details and bank account once. After each job, your share goes to your Stripe account and then to your bank on Stripe's payout schedule, usually within a few business days. You can see your payouts any time from Account → See payouts and earnings.

My waiter didn't show up.

If your waiter hasn't checked in 15 minutes after the start time, open the line in My lines and tap Report no-show. You won't be charged, and your line reopens for other waiters.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open the line and tap Cancel booking. It's free more than 2 hours before the start time. After that, or once your waiter is on the way, it costs 1 hour of waiter pay. Once they're in line, you pay for the time they've waited. The app shows the exact cost before you confirm.

How do I message my waiter or booker?

Once a line is taken, tap Messages on it. Only the two of you can see the conversation.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send a link that lets you choose a new password; it works once and expires in 1 hour. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us.

Can I offer a different rate as a waiter?

Yes. On any open line, tap Offer a different rate. The customer can accept or decline it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account and tap Delete account at the bottom.
