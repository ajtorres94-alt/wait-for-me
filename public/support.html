Common questions

What's the difference between Pick it up for me and Save my spot?

Pick it up for me: your waiter stands in line, buys the item you asked for, and hands it to you, at a meetup or at your door if you offer a drop-off fee. Save my spot: your waiter saves a spot where saving one is normal, like a parade route, a fireworks show, or an outdoor movie lawn, and hands it over when you arrive.

Can I use Save my spot to hold my place in a line?

No. Wait For Me! does not condone or endorse line-cutting. Save my spot can't be used to step into a line ahead of people who are waiting. If you need something from a line, use Pick it up for me and your waiter buys it for you. Waiters: if a booker asks you to hold a place in a line for them, decline and let us know.

What can't waiters buy?

Alcohol, tobacco or vaping products, cannabis, prescriptions, weapons or ammunition, lottery tickets, gift cards, prepaid cards, money orders, and anything illegal or age-restricted. Each request is for one item or order, up to $150 with tax.

How much will I pay?

You pay for your waiter's time from check-in to the purchase (or to the handover, for saved spots), in 15-minute steps, and your waiter earns at least $8 for their time. Each request covers up to 2 hours; if it takes longer, the app asks you to add time. On top of that: a 15% service fee on the time a booking fee ($4.99 for pickups, $2.50 for saved spots), and a $1.99 rush fee if you pick ASAP. For pickups you also pay the item at the receipt price, never more than your limit, plus 3% of the item price for card processing, and any drop-off fee you offered. The app shows the most it can cost before you post.

What if it's sold out?

Your waiter buys your backup choice if you gave one. If that's sold out too, they take a photo showing it, nothing is bought, and you pay only for the time they waited, with no fees.

The price is higher than my limit. What now?

Open the request and tap Raise item limit. Your waiter is told right away, and the hold on your card is adjusted. You can raise it until they buy the item, up to $150.

How do I turn on alerts?

Go to Account → Alerts and tap Turn on alerts. You'll get a notification when your request is claimed, when your item is bought, when it's time to meet, and for new messages, even with the app closed. On iPhone, first add the app to your Home Screen (tap Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Concert merch: does my waiter need a ticket?

If the merch stand is inside the venue, yes. Many tours also sell from a truck or stand outside the gates before doors open. Say which in your request, so waiters know before they claim it.

What are events?

Events are shows and fairs we're focusing on. When you post, pick the event you're at, and waiters already inside get an alert. Waiters: open Find jobs, tap I'm here on the event, and you'll get an alert for each new request there. Tap Claim several at once to grab a few requests at the same stand in one trip.

How fast do waiters get paid?

Pay, including item costs, reaches your Stripe account when the job ends. Cash out instantly from Account → See payouts and earnings (Stripe charges a small fee), or wait for your regular payout.

How do I know what my waiter paid?

When they buy your item, they enter the receipt total and take a photo of the receipt. Open the request in Requests and tap View receipt photo.

Where's my handover code?

It's on your request in Requests. Read it to your waiter in person when you meet. They enter it to hand over your item or spot. Never share it before you're there.

What if I can't meet my waiter?

Your waiter holds your item for up to 4 hours after buying it, so meeting after a show works. For food, meet within 30 minutes so it stays fresh. Message your waiter to work out the spot. If you haven't met by then, they can close the job and you're charged in full, since they already paid for your item. We send you a reminder 20 minutes before it ends. Messages stay open for 7 days so you can arrange to get it.

Why is there a hold on my card?

When a waiter is booked and your request starts within 5 days, we place a temporary hold for the most the job could cost, including the item limit for pickups. When the job ends, we charge only what's owed and release the rest. Your bank may take a few days to remove the released amount from your statement. Charges show up as WAITFORME.

How do I get paid as a waiter?

Go to Account → Set up payouts. Stripe, our payment processor, asks for your details and bank account once. After each job, your pay and the item cost you paid go to your Stripe account and then to your bank on Stripe's payout schedule, usually within a few business days. Don't buy anything until the app shows Payment secured. You can see your payouts any time from Account → See payouts and earnings.

My waiter didn't show up.

If your waiter hasn't checked in 15 minutes after the start time (or 45 minutes after claiming an ASAP request), open the request in Requests and tap Report no-show. You won't be charged, and your request reopens for other waiters.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, until your waiter buys the item. Open the request and tap Cancel booking. It's free more than 2 hours before the start time. After that, or once your waiter is on the way, it costs 30 minutes of waiter time. Once they're in line, you pay for the time they've waited. The app shows the exact cost before you confirm.

How do I message my waiter or booker?

Once a request is taken, tap Messages on it. Only the two of you can see the conversation.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send a link that lets you choose a new password; it works once and expires in 1 hour. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us.

Can I offer a different rate as a waiter?

Yes. On any open job, tap Offer a different rate. The customer can accept or decline it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account and tap Delete account at the bottom.
