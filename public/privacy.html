Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

{{COMPANY}} runs the Wait For Me! app, which connects people who need a spot held in line ("bookers") with people who hold it for them ("waiters"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password. We store your password only in scrambled (hashed) form and can't read it.
  • Profile details you choose to add: the avatar you pick and what you'll be wearing when you wait.
  • Line details: where the line is, what it's for, when to arrive, the estimated wait, the hourly rate, and any notes. We also save map coordinates for each line's location.
  • Job activity: who claimed a line, status updates, place in line, check-in and finish times, swap codes, and the amounts billed.
  • Ratings: the stars and tags you give and receive after a job.
  • Messages: what bookers and waiters send each other about a job. Only the two people on that job can read them, plus our team when resolving a dispute or safety report.

Your location

When a booker posts a line, we look up the map coordinates of the address they typed. When a waiter turns on Near me, the app uses their phone's location only on their device to measure the distance to each line, and doesn't send it to us.

When a waiter taps I'm in line, the app sends their location to us once to confirm they're at the line, because that starts their paid time. We keep only whether the check-in was confirmed and how far it was from the line, not the location itself. We don't track waiters at any other time.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show lines to waiters, share status updates with bookers, and work out what each job costs.
  • To help people find each other at the line, using names, avatars, and outfit descriptions.
  • To keep the community safe and fair, using ratings and job history.
  • To answer your questions and fix problems, and to email you account messages such as password reset links.

We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Waiters browsing open lines see the line's location, time, pay, and the booker's rating. They don't see the booker's name, avatar, or notes.
  • Once a line is claimed, the booker and waiter see each other's name, avatar, and rating, the waiter sees the booker's notes, and the booker sees what the waiter is wearing and whether check-in was confirmed.
  • Only the booker ever sees the swap code.
  • Ratings stay private for 3 days after a job, and an average shows only after someone has 3 ratings.

Services we use

  • Railway hosts the app and its database.
  • Cloudflare manages our domain and forwards email sent to our addresses.
  • Google (Gmail) sends our account emails, such as password reset links, and holds our support inbox.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) receives the address text of new lines to return map coordinates.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's typefaces, so your device connects to Google to load them.
  • When card payments launch, a payment processor will handle card and bank details. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. The app also saves a few preferences on your device, such as whether the opening sound is on and which walkthroughs you've seen.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete account. That removes your name, email, password, avatar, and outfit, and cancels any open lines you posted. Records of finished jobs and ratings stay with the other person, labeled "Deleted user," so their history stays accurate.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to not be treated differently for using these rights. To make a request, contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

Age requirement

Wait For Me! is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
