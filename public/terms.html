What Wait For Me! does

Wait For Me! is a referral service. We connect bookers, who need a place held in a line, with waiters, independent service providers who hold it for them. We provide the app and process payments. We don't perform line-holding ourselves.

Waiters are independent

  • Waiters are independent service providers, not employees, agents, or partners of {{COMPANY}}. They provide their services in their own name.
  • Waiters decide whether to use the app, when, and how often. There are no required hours, shifts, or minimum number of jobs.
  • Waiters choose which lines to take and can turn down any line without penalty. They can accept the booker's listed rate or offer their own rate, which the booker can accept or decline.
  • Our service fee is paid by the booker on top of the agreed rate. It is never deducted from what the waiter is paid.
  • Waiters decide how to do the job, including what to bring and how to communicate with the booker. Status updates and outfit descriptions in the app are optional tools.
  • Waiters are free to work through other apps, competing services, or directly for their own customers.
  • Waiters are responsible for their own taxes and for any license or registration their city or state requires for their work.

Who can use it

  • You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding agreement.
  • Keep your account information accurate and your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.

Bookers agree to

  • Describe the line, timing, and any purchases honestly in the line's notes.
  • Only post lines where holding a spot for someone else is allowed.
  • Arrive for the swap, and share the swap code only in person when taking over the spot.

Waiters agree to

  • Show up for lines they accept, around the time agreed with the booker. If a waiter hasn't checked in 15 minutes after the start time, the booker can report a no-show and the line reopens for others.
  • Tap I'm in line only once they're actually in the line, since paid time starts then. The app checks their location at that moment to confirm it.
  • Follow the venue's rules and staff instructions.
  • Hand over the spot only to the person who gives the correct swap code.
  • Leave any line that becomes unsafe.

Prices, time, and fees

  • The booker lists an hourly rate and an estimated wait. A waiter can accept that rate or offer a different one, and the job is booked at whichever rate both agree to. The estimate, plus any time the booker adds, is the most the job can cost.
  • Paid time runs from the waiter's check-in at the line to the swap. It's billed in 15-minute steps, rounded up, with a 1-hour minimum.
  • Bookers pay a 15% service fee on top of the waiter's pay. Waiters keep 100% of the agreed hourly rate.
  • If the approved time runs out and the booker doesn't add more, the waiter may end the shift and is paid for the full approved time.
  • Cancellations, no-shows, and refunds follow our refund policy, which is shown in the app before you pay.

No guaranteed outcome

We sell time in line, not what's at the front of it. A line can sell out, close, or turn people away. When that happens through no one's fault, the waiter is paid for the time waited and we waive our service fee.

Ratings

Bookers and waiters rate each other after a job. Ratings must be honest and about the job itself. We may remove ratings that are abusive or unrelated to the job.

  • Create fake accounts, post fake lines, or manipulate ratings.
