What Wait For Me! does

Wait For Me! is a referral service. We connect bookers with waiters, independent service providers who do one of two things for them:

  • Pick it up for me. The waiter waits in line, buys the item the booker asked for, and hands it over.
  • Save my spot. The waiter saves a spot where saving one is normal and allowed, like a parade route, a fireworks show, an outdoor movie lawn, a beach or park picnic area, or a campout before an event, and hands it over to the booker.

We provide the app and process payments. We don't do the waiting ourselves.

No line-cutting

Wait For Me! does not condone or endorse line-cutting. Save my spot may not be used to hold a place in a line or queue for someone to step into ahead of people who are waiting. Bookers confirm this each time they post a Save my spot request. Waiters should decline any request to do it and tell us. We remove requests that break this rule and may close the accounts involved.

Waiters are independent

  • Waiters are independent service providers, not employees, agents, or partners of {{COMPANY}}. They provide their services in their own name.
  • Waiters decide whether to use the app, when, and how often. There are no required hours, shifts, or minimum number of jobs.
  • Waiters choose which jobs to take and can turn down any job without penalty. They can accept the booker's listed rate or offer their own rate, which the booker can accept or decline.
  • Our fees are paid by the booker on top of the agreed rate. They are never deducted from what the waiter is paid.
  • Waiters decide how to do the job, including what to bring and how to communicate with the booker. Status updates and outfit descriptions in the app are optional tools.
  • Waiters are free to work through other apps, competing services, or directly for their own customers.
  • Waiters are responsible for their own taxes and for any license or registration their city or state requires for their work.

Who can use it

  • Keep your account information accurate and your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.

Bookers agree to

  • Describe the place, timing, and item honestly, including size, flavor, and a backup choice if you have one.
  • Only ask for one item or order per request, and set an item limit (tax included, up to $150) you're willing to pay.
  • Not ask waiters to buy alcohol, tobacco or vaping products, cannabis, prescriptions, weapons or ammunition, lottery tickets, gift cards, prepaid cards, money orders, or anything illegal or age-restricted.
  • Only use Save my spot where saving a spot for someone else is normal and allowed, and never to skip people waiting in a line.
  • Show up for the handover, and share your code only in person when you receive the item or take over the spot.

Waiters agree to

  • Show up for jobs they accept, around the time agreed with the booker. If a waiter hasn't checked in 15 minutes after the start time (and at least 45 minutes after claiming the job, so ASAP jobs leave time to get there), the booker can report a no-show and the request reopens for others.
  • Check in only once they're actually in line or at the spot, since paid time starts then. The app checks their location at that moment to confirm it.
  • For pickups: buy only what the booker asked for (or their backup), never spend more than the item limit, and record the receipt total with a clear photo of the receipt. If the item is sold out, take a photo that shows it, like the empty shelf or a sold-out sign. Don't buy anything until the app shows the booker's payment is secured.
  • Keep a bought item safe and in the condition it was sold until it's handed over.
  • Follow the venue's rules and staff instructions.
  • Hand over the item or spot only to the person who gives the correct code.
  • Leave any line that becomes unsafe.

Prices, time, and fees

  • The booker lists an hourly rate. A waiter can accept that rate or offer a different one, and the job is booked at whichever rate both agree to. Each request covers up to 2 hours of waiting, plus any time the booker adds, and that's the most the job can cost.
  • Paid time runs from the waiter's check-in to the purchase (for pickups) or to the handover (for saved spots). It's billed in 15-minute steps, rounded up. Waiters earn at least $8 for their time on any job they're paid for.
  • For pickups, the booker also pays the item at the receipt price (never more than the item limit) and any drop-off fee they offered. The waiter is paid back the item cost and keeps all of the drop-off fee.
  • Bookers pay our fees on top: a 15% service fee on the waiter's time, a booking fee ($4.99 for pickups, $2.50 for saved spots), for pickups 3% of the item price to cover card processing, and a $1.99 rush fee on ASAP requests. Waiters keep 100% of the agreed hourly rate.
  • If the approved time runs out before the purchase or handover and the booker doesn't add more, the waiter may end the shift and is paid for the full approved time.
  • Once a pickup is bought, it can't be cancelled. The waiter holds the item for up to 4 hours after the purchase, so meeting after a show works; for food, bookers should meet within 30 minutes. If the booker doesn't meet the waiter within 4 hours, the waiter may close the job after messaging the booker in the app. The booker then pays for the waiter's time, any drop-off fee and our fees, but not the item, and the waiter keeps the item. Waiters can't have more than $200 of unbought item limits claimed at once, and claiming several requests at once is available after a waiter finishes 10 jobs.
  • Cancellations, no-shows, and refunds follow our refund policy, which is shown in the app before you pay. To ask for a refund, tap Report a problem on the job within 14 days.

Payments and payouts

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Bookers save a card (or Apple Pay or Google Pay) before posting a request, and authorize us to charge it for each job they book under these terms.
  • Once a waiter is booked and the job starts within 5 days, we place a temporary hold on the booker's card for the most the job could cost: the approved time plus 30 minutes at the agreed rate, our fees, and for pickups the item limit and any drop-off fee. If the booker adds time, the hold may be replaced with a larger one.
  • When the job ends, we charge only the amount owed under these terms and release the rest of the hold. If nothing is owed, the whole hold is released. Banks can take a few days to remove a released hold from a statement.
  • If a hold or charge is declined, we'll ask the booker to update their card and try again. A waiter may release a job whose payment isn't secured, before checking in, with no penalty.
  • Waiters set up payouts with Stripe, which verifies their identity and collects their bank details. We never see a waiter's full bank or Social Security number. The waiter's share of each charge, including any item cost they paid, is sent to their Stripe account, and Stripe pays it out to their bank on their payout schedule. Waiters are responsible for their own taxes; Stripe may send tax forms.
  • Charges appear on card statements as WAITFORME.

No guaranteed outcome

We sell a waiter's time, not what's at the front of the line. An item can sell out, a store can close, and a venue can turn people away. When that happens through no one's fault, the waiter is paid for the time waited and we waive our fees. The booker isn't charged for an item that wasn't bought.

Ratings

Bookers and waiters rate each other after a job. Ratings must be honest and about the job itself. We may remove ratings that are abusive or unrelated to the job.
